Standardising this for your kitchen

Weigh, don't eyeball: convert every "1 cup" and "1/2 cup" above into grams once you fix your dough size Ã¢â‚¬â€ cup measures vary too much cook to cook to hold food cost.

Portion the toppings, not just the base: pepperoni counts, cheese weight, and protein portions belong on a laminated card at the pizza station, the same way a la carte stations run.
