Preparing for a Restaurant Supervisor Interview

Success in hospitality is not only about serving food â€” it's about leadership, teamwork, customer satisfaction, and operational excellence.

Whether you're applying for your first supervisory role or looking to advance your hospitality career, preparing for common interview questions can help you stand out from other candidates.

This guide covers some of the most frequently asked Restaurant Supervisor interview questions, along with professional sample answers and practical insights to help you interview with confidence.

1. Tell Me About Yourself

Sample Answer:
I have several years of experience in hospitality and food service operations. Throughout my career, I have focused on delivering excellent customer service, supporting team members, and maintaining high operational standards. My experience includes staff scheduling, inventory management, handling customer concerns, and ensuring smooth daily operations. I enjoy leading teams, improving service quality, and creating positive guest experiences that contribute to business growth.
Interview Insight:
Focus on your hospitality experience, leadership skills, and achievements rather than personal details. Keep your answer concise and relevant to the role.

2. Why Do You Want to Work as a Restaurant Supervisor?

Sample Answer:
I enjoy leading teams and creating memorable dining experiences for guests. This role allows me to combine my passion for customer service with my leadership and operational skills. I find satisfaction in motivating staff, solving challenges, maintaining service standards, and helping the business achieve its goals.
Interview Insight:
Employers want to see genuine enthusiasm for leadership and team development, not simply a desire for promotion.

3. What Are the Main Responsibilities of a Restaurant Supervisor?

Sample Answer:
  • Staff supervision and coaching
  • Customer service management
  • Inventory control
  • Food quality monitoring
  • Cost control
  • Staff scheduling
  • Complaint resolution
  • Maintaining hygiene standards
  • Supporting sales growth

4. How Do You Handle Customer Complaints?

Sample Answer:
I listen carefully without interrupting, acknowledge the customer's concerns, apologise for the inconvenience, investigate the issue, and provide a suitable solution promptly. After resolving the situation, I follow up to ensure the guest is satisfied and use the experience as a learning opportunity for the team.

5. How Do You Manage Staff During Busy Hours?

Sample Answer:
I prepare by assigning clear responsibilities before service begins and ensuring everyone understands their role. During busy periods, I monitor workflow, support team members, maintain communication between kitchen and front-of-house teams, and remain calm under pressure to keep service running smoothly.

6. How Do You Control Food Costs?

Sample Answer:
I focus on effective inventory management, accurate portion control, reducing waste, conducting regular stock checks, and following FIFO procedures. I also review supplier pricing and sales reports to improve profitability without compromising quality.

Key Food Cost Control Strategies

  • Portion control
  • Inventory management
  • FIFO stock rotation
  • Waste reduction
  • Supplier negotiations
  • Daily stock checks
  • Menu performance analysis

7. What Would You Do If a Team Member Was Absent?

Sample Answer:
I would quickly assess staffing requirements, reassign responsibilities, support the team where necessary, and contact available backup staff if needed. My priority would be maintaining service standards while supporting team morale.

8. How Would You Increase Restaurant Sales?

Sample Answer:
I would focus on improving the guest experience, encouraging upselling, promoting high-margin menu items, supporting marketing campaigns, implementing loyalty programs, and ensuring staff are trained to identify sales opportunities naturally.

Additional Sales Growth Ideas

  • Suggestive selling techniques
  • Meal bundle promotions
  • Loyalty rewards programs
  • Seasonal menu promotions
  • Social media marketing
  • Encouraging positive online reviews
  • Building relationships with repeat guests

9. How Do You Maintain Hygiene Standards?

Sample Answer:
I ensure all staff follow food safety procedures, personal grooming standards, cleaning schedules, and temperature-control requirements. Regular inspections and training help maintain compliance and protect guest safety.

10. What Is Your Leadership Style?

Sample Answer:
My leadership style is supportive, approachable, and results-focused. I lead by example, communicate clearly, coach team members, and create an environment where accountability, teamwork, and continuous improvement are encouraged.
Leadership Tip:
The most effective restaurant supervisors balance operational excellence with genuine care for both guests and team members.

Additional Interview Questions You May Be Asked

  • How do you motivate your team?
  • How do you resolve conflict between employees?
  • How do you prioritise tasks during a busy shift?
  • Describe a time you improved customer service.
  • How do you train new staff members?
  • What would you do if food quality standards dropped?
