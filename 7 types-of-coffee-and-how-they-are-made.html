The 7 Classic Coffee Drinks
1Espresso

A concentrated 1‑oz (30 ml) shot brewed by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee at high pressure. The foundation for almost every other coffee drink.

🇮🇹 Italy

2Americano

One shot of espresso diluted with hot water (1:2 ratio). Produces a similar strength to drip coffee but with a smoother, more rounded flavour.

🇮🇹 Italy

3Cappuccino

Equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam (1:1:1). Served in a 6‑oz cup and dusted with cocoa or cinnamon. Creamy, airy texture.

🇮🇹 Italy

4Caffè Latte

One or two shots of espresso with a larger amount of steamed milk (1:3 or 1:4) and a thin layer of microfoam. Served in a 8‑10 oz cup; often with latte art.

🇮🇹 Italy

5Macchiato

Espresso "stained" with a small dollop of milk foam (1:1 espresso‑to‑foam ratio). Served in a demitasse cup for a strong, milky‑but‑bold experience.

🇮🇹 Italy

6Mocha

A latte variation with chocolate syrup or powder added to the espresso before the milk. Topped with whipped cream for a dessert‑like drink.

🇺🇸 United States

7Cold Brew

Coarsely ground coffee steeped in cold water for 12–24 hours, then filtered. Served over ice. Low‑acid, smooth, and often stronger in caffeine.

🇺🇸 United States
