Classic Cocktails: A Practical Hospitality Guide

This visual learning guide introduces classic cocktails by their principal spirit: whiskey, vodka, rum, gin and tequila. It is designed as a quick reference for bartenders, hospitality students, restaurant teams and hospitality professionals.

Training note: Cocktail recipes vary by property, region and house specification. Always follow your establishment's approved recipe, responsible-service policy, legal requirements and measured-pour standards.

Why Classic Cocktail Knowledge Matters

Understanding classic builds helps hospitality teams communicate consistently, control portions, train new bartenders, improve guest service and use garnishes appropriately. The poster is intended as a visual reference rather than a substitute for a property's beverage manual or standard operating procedures.

Garnish Guide

The poster highlights common garnishes including lime, lemon, orange, mint, olives, cherries and pineapple. Garnish selection should complement the drink, remain fresh and be prepared hygienically.
