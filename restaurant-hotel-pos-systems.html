POS Systems for Restaurants & Hotels in India

Ten platforms I've watched operators actually run on — five built for restaurants, five built for hotels. What they cost, where they win, where they fall short, and how they hold up when the internet doesn't.

By Nigel A. Thomas — Hospitality Executive & Corporate Trainer

A POS system is a promise made at the busiest possible moment: that a bill will print correctly, a KOT will reach the kitchen before the table's patience runs out, and a night audit will close without a fight. I've stood over enough front desks and expo lines to know that the software brochure and the software's behaviour on a Friday night are two very different documents.

This is a working reference, not a sales pitch. Pricing changes often in this market, so treat the figures below as indicative — always confirm the current quote directly with the vendor before you commit.

Restaurant POS Systems

Five platforms with real footprint across Indian QSRs, cafés, cloud kitchens, and full-service restaurants.

Petpooja

Restaurant POS & Billing

Advantages

  • Very strong aggregator integration (Zomato, Swiggy) with auto order-push
  • Large India-specific support and onboarding network
  • Wide plug-in marketplace: loyalty, inventory, accounting

Disadvantages

  • UI can feel dated compared to newer entrants
  • Some advanced modules cost extra beyond base plan
  • Multi-outlet reporting can lag during peak sync
Indicative pricing: ~₹7,000–₹15,000/outlet/year for base billing, higher tiers for KDS, inventory & multi-outlet add-ons. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Fast table & KOT management, split billing, and direct aggregator order capture at the counter.

BOH Benefit

Centralised inventory, recipe-linked stock deduction, and vendor/purchase tracking.

Offline: billing continues, syncs on reconnect Online: live aggregator sync, cloud reporting

POSist

Enterprise Restaurant Tech

Advantages

  • Strong fit for multi-outlet chains and franchise reporting
  • Robust central kitchen & central inventory module
  • Good analytics dashboard for area/regional managers

Disadvantages

  • Pricing skews toward mid-large operations, less suited to a single small outlet
  • Steeper learning curve for new staff
  • Onboarding and customisation can take longer
Indicative pricing: Custom quote model, typically ₹15,000–₹30,000+/outlet/year depending on modules. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Table management, CRM-linked guest history, and fast bill settlement across formats.

BOH Benefit

Central kitchen production planning and consolidated multi-outlet stock control.

Offline: local billing mode available Online: chain-wide live dashboards

Torqus

Restaurant & Cloud Kitchen POS

Advantages

  • Purpose-built modules for cloud kitchens and delivery-first brands
  • Good real-time menu sync across delivery platforms
  • Reasonably quick setup for single-brand operators

Disadvantages

  • Smaller partner/integration ecosystem than Petpooja or POSist
  • Support responsiveness varies by region
  • Fewer built-in HR/staff scheduling tools
Indicative pricing: ~₹8,000–₹18,000/outlet/year depending on delivery-integration tier. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Quick order entry and table turnover tracking for dine-in plus delivery counters.

BOH Benefit

Recipe costing and ingredient-level stock alerts suited to multi-brand cloud kitchens.

Offline: local order queue retained Online: live multi-brand delivery sync

GoFrugal

Restaurant & Retail POS

Advantages

  • Strong offline-first architecture, popular in tier-2/tier-3 cities
  • Combined retail + restaurant capability useful for hybrid outlets
  • Solid GST-compliant billing and reporting

Disadvantages

  • Interface feels more retail-oriented than restaurant-native
  • Aggregator integrations less seamless than Petpooja
  • Advanced restaurant features may need add-on modules
Indicative pricing: ~₹6,000–₹14,000/outlet/year, license or subscription options. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Reliable billing counter performance even on poor connectivity days.

BOH Benefit

Combined inventory across F&B and retail counters where applicable.

Offline: fully functional billing without internet Online: cloud backup & multi-branch view

Focus POS

Focus Softnet Restaurant Suite

Advantages

  • Deep ERP-linked accounting and finance integration
  • Good fit for restaurant groups already on Focus ERP
  • Configurable for both QSR and fine-dine formats

Disadvantages

  • Overkill and costlier for a single small outlet
  • Implementation timeline longer than lighter-weight POS tools
  • Requires more IT involvement to configure well
Indicative pricing: Custom enterprise quote, typically higher than standalone restaurant POS tools. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Unified guest billing that flows straight into group-level financial reporting.

BOH Benefit

Tight stock-to-ledger reconciliation for finance and F&B cost control teams.

Offline: local terminal mode supported Online: full ERP sync for finance & inventory

Hotel POS & PMS Systems

Five platforms covering front office, F&B point-of-sale, and property management for Indian hotels and resorts.

eZee Absolute

Cloud PMS (eZee Technosys)

Advantages

  • Widely used across independent hotels and small chains in India
  • Strong channel manager and OTA rate/inventory sync
  • Affordable entry tier for boutique properties

Disadvantages

  • Reporting depth can feel limited for large full-service resorts
  • Some modules (POS, spa, banquet) sold separately
  • UI refresh lags behind newer PMS entrants
Indicative pricing: ~₹3,000–₹8,000/room/year depending on module bundle. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Fast check-in/out, folio management, and real-time room status for the front desk.

BOH Benefit

Housekeeping-to-front-office sync and consolidated night audit reporting.

Offline: limited desktop fallback version available Online: live OTA/channel sync, cloud dashboard

IDS Next

Enterprise Hotel PMS

Advantages

  • Deep, mature feature set for large hotels and resort groups
  • Strong multi-property and central reservation capability
  • Long-standing presence with Indian hospitality chains

Disadvantages

  • Higher implementation and training investment
  • Less agile for small independent properties
  • Customisation often needs vendor support involvement
Indicative pricing: Custom enterprise quote; typically higher than boutique-focused PMS. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Group reservations, banquet booking, and multi-property front-desk control from one system.

BOH Benefit

Integrated F&B costing, stores, and finance modules for large-property back office.

Offline: on-premise deployment option available Online: cloud edition with group-wide visibility

Hotelogix

Cloud Hotel Management System

Advantages

  • Clean, modern interface, easy for new front-desk staff to learn
  • Good mobile app support for on-the-go check-in
  • Competitive pricing for independent hotels

Disadvantages

  • Fewer deep customisation options for complex resort operations
  • Some users report slower support turnaround at peak season
  • Advanced F&B/banquet modules less mature than dedicated POS tools
Indicative pricing: ~₹2,500–₹6,000/room/year depending on tier. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Mobile-friendly check-in, reservation calendar, and real-time availability at the desk.

BOH Benefit

Housekeeping task boards and maintenance tracking linked to room status.

Offline: minimal, primarily cloud-dependent Online: real-time OTA sync, mobile access anywhere

RMS Cloud

Global Hotel & Resort PMS

Advantages

  • Very strong for large resorts, groups, and multi-property portfolios
  • Extensive third-party integration marketplace
  • Robust reporting and revenue management tools

Disadvantages

  • Pricing and complexity can be excessive for a small independent hotel
  • Support and onboarding are based overseas, timezone lag possible
  • Steeper learning curve for smaller teams
Indicative pricing: Custom quote, generally positioned at the higher end for the Indian market. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Advanced booking engine and multi-property front-desk consolidation.

BOH Benefit

Deep revenue management and reporting for finance and ownership review.

Offline: limited, cloud-native design Online: strong global distribution and channel reach

Stayflexi

Cloud PMS for Independent Hotels

Advantages

  • Built with dynamic pricing and direct-booking tools in mind
  • Good fit for budget and mid-scale independent hotels/hostels
  • Fast onboarding, modern self-serve setup

Disadvantages

  • Less proven at large full-service resort scale
  • Smaller support footprint compared to legacy PMS vendors
  • Fewer banquet/F&B-specific modules out of the box
Indicative pricing: ~₹2,000–₹5,000/room/year, tiered by feature set. Confirm with vendor.

FOH Benefit

Simple check-in flow with dynamic upsell prompts for room upgrades.

BOH Benefit

Revenue management dashboard that feeds directly into rate strategy decisions.

Offline: minimal, cloud-native design Online: real-time dynamic pricing across all channels

Offline vs Online at a Glance

The one question every operator asks before signing: what happens when the WiFi drops mid-service?

Capability Offline-Capable Systems Cloud-Only Systems
Billing continues during outage Yes — local terminal keeps running No — billing may pause or queue
Data sync after reconnect Automatic sync once online N/A — always live
Multi-property live visibility Delayed until sync Real-time across properties
OTA / aggregator updates Near real-time when online Instant, always-on
Best suited for Locations with unstable internet, single-property operations Metro properties, chains needing centralised control

A pause before choosing a system matters as much as a pause before responding to a guest complaint. Match the platform to your property's connectivity reality, not to the vendor's demo environment — a demo runs on office WiFi; your restaurant floor may not.
