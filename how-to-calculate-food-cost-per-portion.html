1. What Is Food Cost Per Portion?

Food cost per portion is the total cost of ingredients required to prepare one single serving of a dish â€” nothing more, nothing less. It's the raw-material cost baked into every plate that leaves your kitchen.

Why it matters:

  • It protects healthy, sustainable profit margins as ingredient prices move.

  2. Record the exact quantity of each ingredient used.
  3. Note the purchase unit for each item (kg, g, ml, ltr, pcs, etc.).
  4. Find the current cost of each ingredient based on that purchase unit.
  5. Add up every ingredient cost to arrive at the total recipe cost.
