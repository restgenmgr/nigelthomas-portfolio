7 Types of Coffee

Know Your Coffee. Serve It Better. — the 7 essential coffee drinks, in one quick-glance visual.

By Nigel A. Thomas • Hospitality Executive & Corporate Trainer

The 7 Classic Coffee Drinks

1Espresso

A small, strong coffee made by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee under pressure. The base for many other coffee drinks.

Strong & Bold

2Americano

Espresso plus hot water. Has a lighter taste and larger volume than espresso alone.

Smooth & Light

3Cappuccino

Made with espresso, steamed milk and milk foam. Usually served in a smaller cup.

Creamy & Frothy

4Café Latte

Made with espresso and plenty of steamed milk, with a thin layer of foam. Smooth, milky taste.

Smooth & Milky

5Mocha

A combination of espresso and chocolate with steamed milk, often topped with whipped cream or chocolate.

Sweet & Chocolatey

6Macchiato

Espresso with a small amount of steamed milk or milk foam. The word means "marked" or "stained."

Strong & Balanced

7Flat White

Made with espresso and finely textured steamed milk. Less foam than a cappuccino, stronger coffee flavour than a latte.

Rich & Smooth
