Commercial Kitchen Operations Academy

Practical training, operational knowledge and professional resources for chefs, kitchen managers, F&B professionals, supervisors and hospitality teams.

Building Better Commercial Kitchens

The Commercial Kitchen Operations Academy is being developed as a practical hospitality training resource based on real-world food and beverage operations. The objective is simple: provide useful knowledge that can be applied in the kitchen, during service and throughout the daily management of a professional food operation.

Resources will cover food safety, FSSAI requirements, HACCP principles, standard operating procedures, kitchen safety, production planning, inventory, cost control, waste management, staff training and operational leadership.

The Practical Approach

The Academy is designed around practical hospitality operations rather than theory alone. The goal is to explain what should happen, why it matters and how a professional operation can implement the standard consistently.

New modules will be added progressively as the Academy develops.
