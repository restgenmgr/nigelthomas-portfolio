HACCP Kitchen Checklist – Daily Verification Steps

This checklist covers the 7 critical control points that every HACCP‑compliant kitchen must verify before, during, and after service.

✅ Pre‑Service (Start of Shift)

  • Refrigerator & freezer temperatures recorded (below 5°C / -18°C).
  • Receiving area clean and temperature logs checked for incoming goods.
  • Colour‑coded knives and cutting boards verified for correct placement.
  • Handwash stations stocked with soap, paper towels, and sanitizer.
  • No expired or damaged products in storage.

✅ During Service

  • Hot food held at ≥60°C; cold food held at ≤5°C (check every 2 hours).
  • Reheating reaches 75°C (checked with a probe thermometer).
  • Colour‑coded tools remain in their designated stations.
  • No cross‑contamination observed (e.g., raw meat next to ready‑to‑eat).
  • Bins emptied regularly; waste segregation maintained.

✅ Post‑Service (End of Shift)

  • Leftovers cooled from 60°C to 21°C within 2 hours, then to 5°C within 4 hours.
  • All surfaces and equipment sanitised.
  • Waste logs updated; bins cleaned.
  • Temperature logs signed off.
  • Any deviations recorded and corrective actions noted.
Pro Tip: Keep this checklist laminated and posted at your main prep station. Have the CDP or sous chef sign off on it at the end of every shift – it becomes your audit trail.
