Cocktail & Bar Service: Complete Guide

Professional bar Service is an art form. This Guide covers essential bartending techniques, equipment, classic recipes, and Service standards for hospitality professionals.

Essential Bar Equipment

  • Shaker: Boston or Cobbler style for mixing drinks
  • Jigger: Measuring tool for precise pours
  • Bar Spoon: Long-handled spoon for stirring
  • Strainer: Hawthorne or Julep for straining ice
  • Muddler: Pressing herbs and fruits to release flavors

Classic Cocktails

  • Old Fashioned: Whiskey, sugar, bitters
  • Margarita: Tequila, lime juice, triple sec
  • Martini: Gin or vodka, dry vermouth, olive or twist
  • Mojito: Rum, mint, lime, sugar, soda
  • Daiquiri: Rum, lime juice, sugar
  • Negroni: Gin, Campari, sweet vermouth

Pouring Standards

  • Single: 30ml (1 oz)
  • Double: 60ml (2 oz)
  • Free pour: Count 1-4 for a single, 1-8 for a double
  • Always use jigger for accuracy

Glassware

  • Highball: Tall glass for long drinks
  • Rocks: Short glass for spirits on ice
  • Martini: V-shaped for stirred drinks
  • Coupette: Shallow bowl for daisies
  • Wine: Red, white, and champagne glasses

Service Standards

  • Greet guests within 60 seconds
  • Take orders with eye Contact and smile
  • Serve drinks within 2-3 minutes
  • Check back after 2 minutes
  • Clear empty glasses and maintain cleanliness
