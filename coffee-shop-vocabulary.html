Coffee Shop Vocabulary

Essential terms every barista, server and F&B trainee should know — in one quick-glance visual.

16 Essential Coffee Shop Terms

Coffee Cup

For drinking coffee.

Paper Cup

Takeaway cup.

Coffee Maker

Brews coffee.

Teapot

Makes tea.

Espresso

Strong coffee.

Cappuccino

Coffee + milk foam.

Latte

Coffee + milk.

Tea Bag

For making tea.

Milk Frother

Froths milk.

Sugar

Sweetens drink.

Cash Register

To pay the bill.

Barista

Coffee server.

Menu Board

List of drinks/food.

Muffin

Sweet pastry.

Croissant

Buttery bread.

Fridge

Stores drinks.

Spoon

For stirring.

Straw

For cold drinks.

Napkin

For cleaning.

To-Go Bag

For takeaway.

Fluency in the small details builds guest confidence in the big ones.
Know your equipment, know your terms, serve with certainty.
