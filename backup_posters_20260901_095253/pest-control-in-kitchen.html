Pest Control in Hotel Kitchens

Prevention, monitoring, and response protocols

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for Kitchens

Pests are not just a hygiene violation – they are a legal liability. Every HACCP plan must include an IPM strategy. Here is your ready‑to‑implement guide.

🔒 Prevention (The First Line)

  • Seal all gaps around pipes, doors, and windows.
  • Install air curtains and door sweeps.
  • Store all food in sealed containers, off the floor.
  • Keep waste bins sealed and emptied frequently.
  • Maintain strict cleaning schedules (no food debris left overnight).

🔍 Monitoring & Inspection

  • Place sticky traps / pheromone traps around perimeter.
  • Check traps daily – log findings.
  • Inspect incoming deliveries for signs of infestation.
  • Conduct a thorough pest audit every month.
  • Train staff to report droppings, gnaw marks, or live insects immediately.

⚡ Response (When Pests Are Found)

  • Isolate the affected area immediately.
  • Identify the pest species to apply the correct treatment.
  • Use approved pest control contractors (not over‑the‑counter sprays).
  • Deep‑clean and sanitise the affected zone.
  • Review the IPM plan and close the entry point.
Audit Tip: Auditors love to see a Pest Log Book with dates, trap locations, findings, and corrective actions. If you don't have one, start today.
