Sous Chef: The Complete Career Guide for 2026

Welcome to the most comprehensive guide on becoming a Sous Chef in the hospitality industry. As the second-in-command in a professional kitchen, the Sous Chef plays a critical role in ensuring operational excellence, team leadership, and culinary quality.

What is a Sous Chef?

A Sous Chef (pronounced "soo shef") is the second-in-command in a professional kitchen, reporting directly to the Executive Chef. The title comes from French, meaning "under chef" or "deputy chef." This role is one of the most demanding and rewarding positions in the culinary world.

The Sous Chef bridges the gap between the Executive Chef's vision and the kitchen team's daily execution. They are responsible for managing the kitchen in the Executive Chef's absence and ensuring smooth operations during service.

ðŸ”‘ Key Fact: The Sous Chef is often considered the "backbone of the kitchen" - they handle the day-to-day operations while the Executive Chef focuses on menu development and strategic planning.

Key Responsibilities of a Sous Chef

  • Kitchen Management: Overseeing all kitchen operations during service
  • Team Leadership: Supervising, training, and motivating kitchen staff
  • Quality Control: Ensuring consistency and quality across all dishes
  • Inventory Management: Managing stock levels, ordering, and reducing waste
  • Food Safety: Maintaining strict HACCP and hygiene standards
  • Staff Scheduling: Creating and managing kitchen staff rotas
  • Cost Control: Monitoring food costs and portion control
  • Menu Execution: Ensuring the Executive Chef's menu is executed perfectly
  • Kitchen Maintenance: Ensuring equipment is maintained and working properly
  • Training: Developing junior chefs and culinary students
  • Communication: Liaising with front-of-house and management

Required Skills and Qualifications

Culinary Skills

  • Mastery of cooking techniques and methods
  • Strong knowledge of global cuisines and flavor profiles
  • Ability to prepare any dish on the menu
  • Menu planning and recipe development experience
  • Expert knife skills and food preparation techniques

Leadership Skills

  • Team Management: Ability to lead and motivate a diverse team
  • Conflict Resolution: Managing team dynamics and resolving issues
  • Communication: Clear and effective communication across all levels
  • Decision Making: Quick and confident decision-making under pressure
  • Problem Solving: Creative solutions to operational challenges
  • Time Management: Efficient organization and delegation skills

Qualifications

  • Culinary degree or diploma from a recognized institution
  • 8+ years of experience in professional kitchens
  • 5+ years of experience as a Chef de Partie or higher
  • Food safety certification (ServSafe, HACCP, or equivalent)
  • Leadership or management training (preferred)

Average Work Day

A typical day for a Sous Chef is demanding and varied:

  • 6:00 AM - 7:00 AM: Arrive, review daily prep, and check inventory
  • 7:00 AM - 9:00 AM: Supervise breakfast service and morning prep
  • 9:00 AM - 11:00 AM: Staff briefing, scheduling review, and supplier deliveries
  • 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM: Lunch service - leading the team
  • 1:00 PM - 3:00 PM: Break, paperwork, and dinner service planning
  • 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM: Dinner service setup and staff supervision
  • 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM: Dinner service - managing operations
  • 10:00 PM - 11:00 PM: Clean-up, stock-taking, and end-of-day reporting

Interview Tips for Sous Chef Positions

  • Showcase Leadership Experience: Share specific examples of team management and mentoring
  • Demonstrate Business Acumen: Discuss your experience with cost control, inventory, and scheduling
  • Be Prepared for a Practical Test: Many interviews include a cooking challenge
  • Highlight Conflict Resolution: Share examples of handling team conflicts
  • Discuss Menu Development: Showcase your creative contributions
  • Research the Establishment: Understand the restaurant's cuisine and culture
