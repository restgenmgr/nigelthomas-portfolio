Colour Coding of Knives & Cutting Boards

Prevent cross‑contamination with the 7‑colour system

The 7‑Colour Standard for Knives & Boards

  • Red – Raw meat
  • Yellow – Raw poultry
  • Blue – Raw fish & seafood
  • Green – Fruits & vegetables
  • White – Bakery & dairy
  • Brown – Cooked meat
  • Purple – Allergen‑free

Match knife handles to board colours – always use the correct pair.

Rule: A red knife must be used with a red board – never mix colours.
