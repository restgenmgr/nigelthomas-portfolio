The complete guide to hotel operations, guest satisfaction, SOPs, and leadership.

What Is a MOD (Manager on Duty)?

A Manager on Duty (MOD) is the manager who is fully accountable for the smooth operation of the hotel during a specific shift. In branded hotels, the MOD represents senior management and ensures that guest service standards, safety procedures, and operational policies are consistently followed.

One Shift, One Responsibility, Total Excellence.

Key Responsibilities

  • Ensure smooth operations in all areas of the hotel.
  • Handle guest complaints promptly.
  • Welcome VIP and VVIP guests.
  • Coordinate Front Office, Housekeeping, F&B, Engineering, and Security.
  • Ensure compliance with SOPs and brand standards.
  • Handle emergencies effectively.
  • Prepare daily operational reports.

Who Can Be MOD?

  • Front Office Manager
  • Housekeeping Manager
  • F&B Manager
  • Duty Manager
  • Assistant Operations Manager
  • Resident Manager

Conclusion

The MOD is the operational leader who ensures guest satisfaction, safety, and hotel standards throughout the shift.
