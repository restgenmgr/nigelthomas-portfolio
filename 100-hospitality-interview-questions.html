🎤 100 Hospitality Interview Questions & Answers

Master your next hospitality interview with these real questions asked by hiring managers at hotels, restaurants, cruise lines, and resorts.

General Interview Questions

1. Why did you choose a career in hospitality?

Sample Answer: "I've always been passionate about creating memorable experiences for people. Every day brings new challenges and opportunities to make someone's day better."

2. How do you handle a guest who is yelling at you?

Sample Answer: "I stay calm, listen actively, acknowledge their feelings, apologize, take ownership, and resolve the issue quickly."

Food & Beverage Questions

21. How do you handle a food allergy request?

Sample Answer: "I note the allergy, communicate clearly with the kitchen, avoid cross-contamination, and confirm with the guest before serving."

Front Office Questions

41. How do you handle a guest who arrives before check-in time?

Sample Answer: "I check if a room is ready. If not, I offer to store their luggage and invite them to enjoy our lobby."

Pro Tips

  • Always use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
  • Research the property before your interview
  • Prepare specific examples with numbers and metrics
