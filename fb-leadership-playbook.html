The F&B Leadership Playbook

15 Interview Questions & 10 Must-Know KPIs

Core Interview Questions Q1 – Q10
Q1.
Tell me about yourself. Clear intro → relevant experience → why you are the right fit.
Q2.
Why are you interested in this role? Connect your experience to what specifically attracts you to this role.
Q3.
Why should we hire you? Prove it with real achievements and results from past roles.
Q4.
What are your strengths? Choose 2–3 strengths that match the job and back each one with a real example.
Q5.
Tell me about a challenge you faced. Focus on what you did, the outcome, and what you learned – not just the problem.
Q6.
Describe a conflict with a coworker. Show empathy, focus on the solution, and explain how you worked together to resolve it.
Q7.
Tell me about a time you failed. Own the mistake, explain what changed afterward, and focus on the lesson learned.
Q8.
What is your biggest weakness? Choose a genuine weakness, explain what you are doing about it, and show your progress.
Q9.
Why are you leaving your current job? Keep it positive. Focus on growth and new opportunities – never on complaints.
Q10.
Where do you see yourself in 5 years? Show ambition, but make sure your goals connect naturally to this opportunity.
Advanced Leadership Questions Q11 – Q15
Q11.
How do you handle a difficult guest? Acknowledge their frustration, listen without interrupting, apologise sincerely, take ownership of the solution, and follow up to ensure satisfaction.
Q12.
How do you prioritise tasks during a busy shift? Explain your system for triaging tasks, how you delegate to your team, and how you maintain quality standards under pressure.
Q13.
How do you motivate your team? Lead by example, recognise wins publicly, create growth opportunities, and build a supportive team culture.
Q14.
What is your approach to training new staff? Describe your step‑by‑step process: shadowing, hands‑on practice, structured feedback, and ongoing support beyond the initial training period.
Q15.
How do you handle mistakes made by your team members? Address issues privately, focus on solutions rather than blame, use mistakes as coaching moments, and maintain team morale.
10 Must-Know KPIs for F&B Leaders Track what matters. Act on what drives profit.
1. Labor Cost % Cost / Revenue × 100
2. REVPASH Revenue / (Seats × Hours)
3. Table Turnover Parties Seated / Tables
4. Break-Even Revenue Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin
5. Employee Turnover % Leavers / Avg Headcount × 100
6. Food Waste % Waste Cost / Purchases × 100
7. Gross Profit Margin (Revenue – COGS) / Revenue × 100
8. Booking Conversion % Confirmations / Inquiries × 100
9. Complaint Resolution Time Avg time to resolve
10. Upsell Success Rate Upsells / Transactions × 100

The Bigger Lesson

A KPI is only useful when it leads to an action.

A NUMBER tells you what happened.
ANALYSIS helps you understand why.
LEADERSHIP decides what to do next.

That is where F&B management moves from simply controlling operations to actually leading the business.
