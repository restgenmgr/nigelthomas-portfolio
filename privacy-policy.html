Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Graveyard Workforce ("we," "us," or "our") provides an AI-powered operations layer for hotel shift relief coverage. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — when you submit a pilot request or contact form, we collect your name, email address, hotel or company name, and any message you include.
  • Automatically collected information — we use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use this site, including pages viewed, time on site, and general location (derived from IP address, not stored individually).
  • Cookies — this site may use standard cookies required for analytics functionality. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to pilot requests and inquiries
  • Communicate with you about our product and services
  • Understand and improve how visitors use our website
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with third-party service providers who help us operate this website and process inquiries (such as hosting and analytics providers), under obligations to protect your data. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact and pilot request information for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain a record of business communications, unless you request deletion sooner.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us through our contact page. You may also opt out of analytics tracking using your browser's cookie settings or standard browser extensions.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our contact page.
