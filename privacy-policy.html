Welcome to nigelthomas.live. This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, and protect your information when you visit my website.

1. Information I Collect

I use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymized data about website traffic. This includes:

GA4 does NOT collect personally identifiable information (PII) like your name, address, or email unless you voluntarily provide it via the contact form.

2. Cookies

This website uses cookies to improve user experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. GA4 uses first-party cookies to distinguish unique users and sessions. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect site functionality.

3. Google AdSense

I use Google AdSense to display advertisements. AdSense may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history across other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. AdSense also uses third-party vendors (e.g., Google DoubleClick) that may place cookies on your browser.

If you contact me through the contact form, I collect your name, email address, and message content. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry. I do not sell or share this data with third parties.

5. Third-Party Links

My website may contain links to external sites (e.g., LinkedIn). I am not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their privacy policies independently.

6. Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months. Contact form submissions are retained for 12 months or as long as necessary to fulfill your request.
