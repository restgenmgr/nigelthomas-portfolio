Restaurant KPIs Every Manager Should Track

By Nigel A. Thomas • Hospitality Executive & Corporate Trainer

What Gets Measured Gets Improved

Running a restaurant on instinct alone only gets you so far. The managers who consistently outperform their peers are the ones who track the numbers — daily, weekly, and month-on-month — and act on what those numbers tell them. Here are the 10 KPIs I put in front of every manager I train.

1. Sales Growth

  • Daily sales
  • Weekly sales
  • Month-on-month growth

2. Food Cost

  • Monitor cost trends
  • Reduce wastage
  • Improve yield

3. Beverage Cost

  • Track pour cost
  • Control variance
  • Minimize wastage

4. Labour Cost

  • Staff productivity
  • Overtime control
  • Smart scheduling

5. Guest Satisfaction

  • Online reviews
  • Guest feedback
  • Complaint resolution

6. Average Service Time

  • Order to serve
  • Table turnaround time

7. Table Turnover

  • Covers per table
  • Peak hour utilization

8. Average Spend Per Guest

  • Upselling performance
  • Menu mix

9. Food Wastage

  • Kitchen waste
  • Plate waste
  • Expiry loss

10. Repeat Guest Ratio

  • Returning guests
  • Loyalty performance
What gets measured gets improved. Successful restaurant managers don’t just manage operations — they manage numbers.

Start With What You Can Measure

You don’t need all 10 KPIs live on day one. Pick the two or three that move the needle most for your outlet right now, get your team measuring them consistently, then build outward. Discipline in tracking beats ambition in scope every time.
