10 Real Restaurant Operational Challenges & Practical Solutions

By Nigel Thomas | Hospitality Operations | 30+ Years Experience

This guide expands the ten operational challenges shown in the infographic into practical management strategies suitable for restaurants, cafés, hotels and resorts.

The 10 Challenges

  1. Long Food Waiting Time
  2. Staff Shortage During Peak Hours
  3. High Food Wastage
  4. Guest Complaints
  5. Inventory Variance
  6. Low Staff Motivation
  7. Online Order Delays
  8. Poor Hygiene Standards
  9. Low Average Guest Spend
  10. Negative Online Reviews

Management Approach

Each challenge should be managed through SOPs, daily KPI reviews, coaching, communication, forecasting and continuous improvement. Restaurant leaders who monitor labour cost, food cost, guest satisfaction, ticket time and hygiene compliance consistently outperform those who react only after complaints arise.
