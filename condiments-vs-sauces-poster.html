How to Use This Poster

This poster is designed as a quick training reference for the pass, the back office, or a staff noticeboard — print it, pin it, or keep it on a tablet in the kitchen or service area. It gives new team members a fast, memorable way to tell a sauce from a condiment when a guest or a trainer asks the difference.

The Easiest Way to Remember

A sauce complements the dish — it's usually prepared and plated with the food. A condiment customises the dish — the guest decides how much to use, typically at the table. Some products can be either, depending on how they're prepared, served and used.
