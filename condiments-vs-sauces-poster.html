Hospitality Metrics · F&B Terminology Part 12

CondimentsvsSauces

Are they really the same?

🥄

Sauce

A sauce is generally prepared to accompany, enhance, or add flavour and moisture to a dish.

Examples
  • Béchamel
  • Demi-glace
  • Tomato sauce
  • Hollandaise
  • Mushroom sauce
  • Pepper sauce
🍽 Usually an integral part of the dish, plated with it.
🍶

Condiment

A condiment is added according to the guest's personal preference, usually at the table or while eating.

Examples
  • Ketchup
  • Mustard
  • Mayonnaise
  • Pickles
  • Chutneys
  • Relishes
👤 The guest decides how much to use.
The easiest way to remember

Sauce

Complements the dish

VS

Condiment

Customises the dish

However, there can be overlap. Some products can be described as either a sauce or a condiment, depending on how they are prepared, served, and used.
🍔
Burger + prepared sauce, plated with it
= SAUCE
🍔
Burger + ketchup / mustard on the side
= CONDIMENT
💡

F&B Tip: Understanding these small terminology differences can make a big difference in professional communication, menu planning, and service standards.
