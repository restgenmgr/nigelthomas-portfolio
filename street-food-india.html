Food trucks and mobile kitchens have become the face of modern street dining in India. Cities like Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Goa are witnessing a rapid increase in gourmet trucks serving fusion dishes, Asian cuisine, healthy bowls, and experiential food concepts.

Chinese Street Food

Chinese street food remains the most dominant Asian influence in India. Indo-Chinese cuisine has become an entire category of its own through dishes like:

  • Hakka noodles
  • Schezwan fried rice
  • Manchurian bowls
  • Chilli chicken
  • Street-style momos

Vietnamese Cuisine

Vietnamese cuisine is becoming popular among health-conscious urban consumers due to its light ingredients, fresh herbs, soups, and rice-paper preparations.

  • Pho noodle soups
  • Rice paper rolls
  • Lemongrass chicken
  • Fresh herb salads

Cambodian Cuisine

Cambodian food remains niche but has growing interest among travelers and culinary explorers.

  • Coconut curries
  • Grilled seafood skewers
  • Fermented spice flavors
  • Rice-based street snacks

Japanese Street Food

Japanese cuisine has gained premium appeal in India due to strong branding and social media visibility.

  • Ramen bowls
  • Sushi counters
  • Takoyaki stalls
  • Tempura snacks
  • Matcha desserts

Filipino Street Food

Filipino cuisine is slowly emerging in metro cities through fusion kitchens and food trucks.

  • Chicken adobo
  • BBQ skewers
  • Halo-halo desserts
  • Garlic rice bowls
