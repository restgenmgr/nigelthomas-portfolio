The 10 Types of Service
1American Service (Plate Service)

Food is portioned and plated in the kitchen and served directly to the guest.

2English Service

Food is presented on platters and served to guests, often with the host or server assisting.

3French Service

Food is presented at the table, with guests traditionally serving themselves or being assisted by the server.

4Russian Service

Food is attractively arranged on platters and presented to the guest before being served by the waiter.

5Gueridon Service

Food is prepared, finished, carved or flambÃ¯Â¿Â½ed beside the guest's table using a service trolley.

6Silver Service

Food is served from a platter onto the guest's plate using a service spoon and fork.

7Buffet Service

Guests select food from a buffet setup, while F&B staff focus on assistance, replenishment and service standards.

8Family Service

Food is placed on the table in shared dishes, allowing guests to serve themselves.

9Counter Service

Guests place their order and receive food or beverages at a counter.

10Room Service

Food and beverages are delivered and served directly in the guest's room.
