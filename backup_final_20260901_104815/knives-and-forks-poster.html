How to Use This Poster

This poster is designed as a quick training reference for the floor, the back office, or a staff noticeboard — print it, pin it, or keep it on a tablet for pre-shift briefings. In professional hospitality, cutlery is not just about eating; the size, shape and position of each piece communicates the course being served and signals attention to detail to the guest.

Quick Rule of Thumb

Knife = cutting, spreading, separating. Fork = picking, holding, eating. Once new team members internalise this simple split, matching the right piece to the right course becomes second nature.
