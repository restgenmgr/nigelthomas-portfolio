How to Use This Poster

This poster is designed as a quick training reference for the bar, the back office, or a staff noticeboard — print it, pin it, or keep it on a tablet behind the counter. Each drink shows its key build (espresso, milk, foam, syrup or spirit) at a glance, so new team members can match a guest's order to the right recipe in seconds.
