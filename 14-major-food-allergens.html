The 14 Major Allergens
1Celery

Includes celery stalks, leaves, seeds and celeriac.

Found in soups, salads, stocks, seasonings.

2Cereals Containing Gluten

Includes wheat, rye, barley, oats, spelt and kamut.

Found in bread, pasta, cakes, pastries, beer, sauces.

3Crustaceans

Includes prawns, shrimp, crab, lobster and crayfish.

Found in seafood dishes, sauces, soups.

4Eggs

Includes eggs from all birds.

Found in bakery products, mayonnaise, desserts, sauces, pasta.

5Fish

Includes all fish species and fish-derived products.

Found in seafood dishes, fish sauce, stocks.

6Lupin

A legume often used in flour and gluten-free products.

Found in bakery and specialty food items.

7Milk

Includes milk, cream, butter, cheese, yogurt and dairy products.

Found in desserts, sauces, soups, baked goods.

8Molluscs

Includes mussels, oysters, clams, scallops, squid and octopus.

Found in seafood dishes, soups, sauces.

9Mustard

Includes mustard seeds, powder and prepared mustard.

Found in dressings, marinades, sauces, curries.

10Tree Nuts

Includes almonds, walnuts, cashews, hazelnuts, pistachios, pecans, Brazil nuts, macadamia nuts.

Found in desserts, snacks, sauces, baked goods.

11Peanuts

Includes peanuts (groundnuts) and peanut-based products.

Found in peanut butter, sauces, desserts, snacks.

12Sesame

Includes sesame seeds, sesame oil and tahini.

Found in breads, Asian dishes, dressings, hummus.

13Soybeans (Soya)

Includes soybeans and soy-based products.

Found in soy sauce, tofu, soy milk, edamame, miso, processed foods.

14Sulphur Dioxide & Sulphites

Chemical preservatives used in some foods and drinks.

Found in dried fruits, wine, beer, vinegar, pickles, juices, processed foods.

Our Responsibility as Hospitality Professionals
? Ask Always ask guests about food allergies.
?? Inform Inform the kitchen immediately and clearly.
?? Verify Verify ingredients and read labels when unsure.
?? Prevent Prevent cross-contamination during preparation and service.
??? Serve Safely Serve the correct dish to the correct guest.

Food allergies are medical conditions, not dietary preferences.
Accuracy, communication and care can save lives.

When in doubt, check. When unsure, ask. When alerted, act.
