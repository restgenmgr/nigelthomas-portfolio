Role of a Host in a Hotel / Restaurant
01

Welcomes guests with a smile — the first and most lasting impression of the entire visit.

02

Greets and escorts guests to their table, reading the room to seat comfortably and efficiently.

03

Manages reservations and the waiting list, balancing guest expectations against real table turn times.

04

Coordinates with the service team so covers, courses and section loads stay in sync.

05

Ensures guests have a pleasant dining experience from arrival to the moment they leave.

A great host creates an excellent first impression
and ensures guest satisfaction.
