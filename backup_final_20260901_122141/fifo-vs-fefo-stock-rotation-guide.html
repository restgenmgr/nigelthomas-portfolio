FIFO · LIFO · FEFO

Stock Rotation Rules — Every Store Manager Must Know Protecting Food Quality · Reducing Waste · Controlling Cost · Ensuring Safety

FIFO

Queue
First In, First Out — the first item in is the first item out.
IN
  • Day 1 Milk → DAY 1
  • Day 2 Yogurt → DAY 2
  • Day 3 Juice → DAY 3
⏳ Like a queue — first come, first served
✦ Example — need 1 item
After taking 1:
Day 1 Day 2 Day 3
Remaining: Yogurt (Day 2) · Juice (Day 3)
Best for Dry goods, canned items, non‑perishables
Remember FIFO = Queue

LIFO

Stack
Last In, First Out — the last item in is the first item out.
IN
  • Day 1 Milk → DAY 1
  • Day 2 Yogurt → DAY 2
  • Day 3 Juice → DAY 3
📚 Like a stack — last placed, first removed
✦ Example — need 1 item
After taking 1:
Day 1 Day 2 Day 3
Remaining: Milk (Day 1) · Yogurt (Day 2)
Best for Long shelf life, non‑perishable goods, some chemicals
Remember LIFO = Stack

FEFO

Expiry First · Live
First Expired, First Out — nearest expiry used first, regardless of arrival.
IN (auto‑generated expiry)
  • Day 1 Milk Exp: --
  • Day 2 Yogurt Exp: --
  • Day 3 Juice Exp: --
⏰ FEFO: Uses the item with the earliest expiry first — even if it arrived after others.
(Expiry dates update automatically to today + days)
✦ Example — need 1 item
After taking 1:
Day 1 Day 2 Day 3
Remaining: Milk · Juice
Yogurt (earliest expiry) used first.
Best for Perishables: meat, dairy, seafood, produce, prepared food
Remember FEFO = Expiry Date
