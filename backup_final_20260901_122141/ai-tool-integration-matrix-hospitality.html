The AI Tool Integration Decision Matrix
for Hospitality

A working framework for hotel, resort, and F&B leaders to evaluate, pilot, and scale AI tools with clarity and confidence — before they create fragmentation across properties or departments.

By Nigel A. Thomas — Hospitality Executive & Corporate Trainer

Framework structure adapted from The Decision Matrix Series by Abhinav Prathivadi, reframed here for hotel, resort, and food & beverage operations.

Every season brings a new wave of AI tools pitched to hotels and resorts — forecasting engines, guest-messaging bots, F&B ordering assistants, scheduling copilots. The hard part isn't finding one that looks promising. It's making sure it actually fits how a property runs, without creating a new silo, a compliance gap, or a tool nobody on the floor ends up using. This matrix walks through five categories, each examined through Who / What / Where / When / Why, so you can score any AI tool before it goes anywhere near a live shift.
