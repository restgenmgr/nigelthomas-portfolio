Restaurant Fire Safety: Complete Training Guide for FOH & BOH Staff

Fire safety is one of the most important responsibilities in restaurant and hotel operations. A small kitchen fire can become a major emergency within minutes if staff do not understand fire prevention, extinguisher selection, evacuation procedures and emergency communication.

Why Fire Safety Training Matters in Restaurants

Restaurants contain several potential fire hazards at the same time. Open flames, cooking oil, gas equipment, electrical appliances, extraction systems, paper products, cleaning chemicals, wooden furniture and crowded dining areas can all contribute to fire risk.

For this reason, fire safety cannot be treated as the responsibility of the security department or maintenance team alone. Every employee should understand what to do when a fire is detected.

Golden rule: Staff safety comes before property, equipment, food, stock or revenue. Never place yourself or another employee in unnecessary danger to save property. ```
