Cleaning & Sanitising Schedule for Professional Kitchens

This template covers daily, weekly, and monthly cleaning duties. Assign each task to a specific person or station, and sign off on completion.

🧹 Daily Cleaning Tasks

  • Wipe down all food‑contact surfaces (counters, cutting boards).
  • Clean and sanitise all knives and utensils.
  • Empty and wash all waste bins.
  • Mop floors – use separate mops for different zones.
  • Clean handwash stations and top up soap/towels.
  • Dishwashing machine – descale and check chemical levels.

🧽 Weekly Cleaning Tasks

  • Deep‑clean ovens, grills, and fryers.
  • Degrease exhaust hoods and baffle filters.
  • Wipe down walls, behind equipment, and under counters.
  • Clean and sanitise walk‑in cooler shelves and drawers.
  • Inspect and clean ice machines.

🛠️ Monthly Cleaning Tasks

  • Strip and deep‑clean floor drains.
  • Service and descale dishwashers.
  • Check and clean ventilation system.
  • Inspect all equipment for damage or wear.
  • Rotate and check stock for expired items.
Best Practice: Assign a "sign‑off" column for each task. Have the CDP verify completion before the shift ends. This becomes your legal record during an audit.
