North Indian cuisine is known for its rich, creamy gravies, extensive use of dairy, and aromatic spices. This ï¿½ ï¿½ Guide covers the essential dishes, cooking techniques, and ï¿½ ï¿½ Service standards.

?? Signature Dishes

  • Butter Chicken: Creamy tomato-based gravy with tender chicken pieces
  • Dal Makhani: Slow-cooked black lentils with butter and cream
  • Biryani: Fragrant rice layered with spiced meat and saffron
  • Rogan Josh: Slow-cooked lamb curry from Kashmir
  • Chole: Spiced chickpea curry, popular street food

??? Essential Spices

  • Garam Masala: Aromatic spice blend with cinnamon, cardamom, cloves
  • Cumin: Earthy, warm flavor used in most dishes
  • Coriander: Fresh and citrusy, used as ground or whole seeds
  • Turmeric: Bright yellow, earthy flavor and health benefits
  • Red Chili: Heat and color to dishes

?? Breads

  • Naan: Tandoor-baked leavened bread
  • Roti/Chapati: Whole wheat unleavened bread
  • Paratha: Layered, pan-fried flatbread
  • Poori: Deep-fried puffy bread

?? Rice & Biryani

  • Basmati rice is the preferred variety
  • Biryani is layered with saffron, caramelized onions, and mint
  • Best served with raita (yogurt sauce) or salan (gravy)

?? Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun: Fried milk balls soaked in rose syrup
  • Rasmalai: Soft paneer dumplings in sweetened milk
  • Kulfi: Traditional Indian ice cream with cardamom and pistachios
